SpireHDL — automated RTL-to-GDS design-space exploration

A framework that generates GEMM core variants from a hardware DSL, pushes every one through the open-source flow, and lets Bayesian optimization and GNN surrogates decide where to spend synthesis time.

  • 700+INT4/INT8 4×4×4 GEMM variants through Yosys + OpenROAD on IHP SG13G2 130 nm
  • GNNsurrogate models predicting power, performance and area ahead of place-and-route
  • 1 chipGEMM test chip taped out by the team, with full backend and chip-level integration
Read more

DSL-generated RTL beat hand-written SystemVerilog baselines on the open-source flow, and outperformed a commercial flow in parts of the design space. A cross-toolchain study — open-source vs. commercial EDA for GEMM DSE — is the paper direction. Presented to Huawei Research and Prof. Benini.

ETH IIS · Mini project2026

MX KV-cache compression & MXCore

Microscaling-quantized KV cache for MobileLLM-125M, benchmarked on three hardware paths — and an RTL fix that removed the software bottleneck.

  • 1.94×KV-cache compression at +0.45 perplexity (MXFP8)
  • 18–28×GEMM speedup on native MXCore attention
  • 2.2×fewer cycles per attention tile, 373,853 → 173,322
Read more

Compared FP16 on 8 cores, MX with software dequant, and viDMA + MXCore across MXFP8 and MXFP4. Extending the viDMA RTL to write MXCore's data and scale planes directly removed a 208,200-cycle software bridge. With Lars van Dorp.

02 / Research

Where I'm heading

Three threads I keep pulling on, and the questions I'd most like to work on next.

thread_01

Agentic chip design

Multi-agent systems where RTL generation, verification and PPA feedback from the EDA flow talk to each other — so a designer steers tradeoffs instead of babysitting tools.

thread_02

Hardware for LLM inference

Memory is the bottleneck. Microscaling formats, KV-cache compression and dataflows that keep compute fed on small, efficient accelerators.

thread_03

Open heterogeneous hardware

RISC-V platforms like X-HEEP and PULP as building blocks, so anyone with a model can generate a chip for it and co-optimize the two.
